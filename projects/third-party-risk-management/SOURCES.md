# Reference sources — Third-Party Risk Management

## Supply-chain and third-party risk frameworks
- [NIST SP 800-161 Rev. 1](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/161/r1/upd1/final) — cybersecurity supply-chain risk management practices.
- [NIST Cybersecurity Framework 2.0](https://www.nist.gov/cyberframework) — enterprise cyber-risk governance and outcomes.
- [ISO/IEC 27036 overview](https://www.iso.org/standard/59648.html) — information security for supplier relationships.
- [Cloud Security Alliance Cloud Controls Matrix](https://cloudsecurityalliance.org/research/cloud-controls-matrix) — cloud control domains and assessment mapping.

## Evidence, identity and operations
- [NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5](https://csrc.nist.gov/pubs/sp/800/53/r5/upd1/final) — control catalogue for access, audit, incident response and supplier risk.
- [OpenID Connect Core](https://openid.net/specs/openid-connect-core-1_0.html) — identity federation reference.
- [OpenTelemetry](https://opentelemetry.io/docs/) — monitoring of service dependencies and operational evidence.

## Apply these references to this project
Assess vendors by service criticality, data sensitivity, connectivity, concentration and fourth-party exposure. Link each finding to dated evidence, accountable owners and remediation. Avoid treating a questionnaire or certification as a substitute for ongoing monitoring and contract-specific due diligence.
