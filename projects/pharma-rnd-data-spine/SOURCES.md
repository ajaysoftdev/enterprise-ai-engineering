# Reference sources — Pharma R&D Data Spine

## Scientific data standards and traceability
- [CDISC Standards](https://www.cdisc.org/standards) — clinical and nonclinical research data standards.
- [HL7 FHIR](https://hl7.org/fhir/) — healthcare and clinical data interoperability where relevant.
- [FAIR Principles](https://www.go-fair.org/fair-principles/) — findable, accessible, interoperable and reusable research data.
- [W3C PROV-O](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/) — provenance for experiments, samples, methods, results and derived datasets.

## Regulated records and data integrity
- [FDA 21 CFR Part 11](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-21/chapter-I/subchapter-A/part-11) — US electronic records and electronic signatures regulation; applicability depends on intended use and regulatory context.
- [FDA Data Integrity and Compliance With Drug CGMP](https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/data-integrity-and-compliance-drug-cgmp-questions-and-answers-guidance-industry) — data-integrity expectations for relevant CGMP records.
- [OpenLineage](https://openlineage.io/docs/) — technical lineage for data pipelines.

## Apply these references to this project
Preserve original source records, instrument/method/protocol versions, timestamps, sample identity and transformation lineage. Distinguish research data management from regulated electronic-record systems; do not claim Part 11 compliance without validated controls, procedures and intended-use assessment.
