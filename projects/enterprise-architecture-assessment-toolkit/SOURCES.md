# Reference sources — Enterprise Architecture Assessment Toolkit

## Enterprise architecture and modelling
- [The Open Group TOGAF Standard](https://www.opengroup.org/togaf) — enterprise architecture method, content and governance.
- [ArchiMate](https://www.opengroup.org/archimate-forum/archimate-overview) — modelling language for business, application and technology architecture.
- [NIST Enterprise Architecture](https://www.nist.gov/itl/enterprise-architecture) — federal EA guidance and reference materials.
- [OMG BPMN 2.0](https://www.omg.org/spec/BPMN/2.0/) — process modelling notation.
- [C4 Model](https://c4model.com/) — software architecture diagrams at multiple levels.

## Governance and decision records
- [The Open Group Architecture Governance](https://pubs.opengroup.org/togaf-standard/architecture-governance/) — architecture governance concepts.
- [ADR GitHub](https://adr.github.io/) — lightweight architecture decision record practice.
- [W3C PROV-O](https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/) — traceability from evidence to assessment findings and decisions.

## Apply these references to this project
Connect strategy and business drivers to capabilities, value streams, information, applications, integrations, technology, risks and initiatives. Keep evidence links and confidence visible. Record options, trade-offs, dependencies and decision owners; do not present an assessment score as objective fact without a documented scoring model.
