# Reference sources — Predictive Life Sciences Supply Chain

## Supply-chain traceability and interoperability
- [GS1 EPCIS and CBV standards](https://www.gs1.org/standards/epcis) — event-based visibility and traceability for products, lots and shipments.
- [FDA Drug Supply Chain Security Act](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-supply-chain-security-act-dscsa) — US pharmaceutical traceability requirements and guidance; jurisdiction-specific applicability must be assessed.
- [HL7 FHIR](https://hl7.org/fhir/) — healthcare interoperability reference where clinical/site data exchange is in scope.

## Data and AI governance
- [NIST AI Risk Management Framework](https://www.nist.gov/itl/ai-risk-management-framework) — risk management for predictive decision support.
- [OpenLineage](https://openlineage.io/docs/) — lineage for ingestion and feature pipelines.
- [OpenTelemetry](https://opentelemetry.io/docs/) — operational monitoring across event and prediction services.

## Apply these references to this project
Model material, lot, batch, shipment, site and quality-status events with source identifiers and timestamps. Separate predicted risk from confirmed quality disposition. Keep planners accountable for operational actions and retain feature, model and data-version evidence for each prediction.
